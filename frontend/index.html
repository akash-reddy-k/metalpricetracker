What is the GST on gold in India in 2026?

GST on gold in India is 3% on the gold value and 5% on making charges.

What is the import duty on gold in India in 2026?

The basic customs duty on gold imported into India is 15% in 2026.

How is gold price calculated in INR?

The international spot price (USD per troy ounce) is converted to INR using the live USD/INR exchange rate, then divided by 31.1035 to get price per gram.

What is the difference between 24K and 22K gold price?

24K gold is 99.9% pure and the most expensive per gram. 22K gold is 91.6% pure, costing roughly 91.6% of 24K gold for the same weight, before making charges.
